What is Vitalium?

Vitalium is a workplace wellness app. You connect your Strava account, and your workouts automatically earn points. Points appear on your company's weekly leaderboard and can be redeemed for gift cards.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Open the app, tap Get started, then Connect with Strava, and approve access on Strava. You need a Strava account.

My workouts are not showing up

Activities sync from Strava after you save them there. Check the activity is recorded in Strava, then reopen Vitalium. If it still does not appear after an hour, contact us.

How do points and rewards work?

Each workout earns points based on its duration and intensity, up to a weekly limit set for your company. Points you earn each week can be redeemed for gift cards in the Rewards tab. Points cannot be bought.

Who can see my information?

Colleagues at your company see your name and weekly points on the leaderboard. See our privacy policy for the full details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Profile tab and tap Delete account. You can also request deletion by email.

Vitalium uses the Strava API but is not developed, sponsored, or endorsed by Strava.
