Privacy Policy

Vitalium · Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Vitalium app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Vitalium is operated in South Africa and this policy follows the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

Vitalium is operated by [YOUR FULL NAME], who is the responsible party and the Information Officer for Vitalium. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Vitalium is a workplace wellness app. Your employer takes part in Vitalium, and you join by connecting your Strava account. Vitalium uses the Strava API but is not developed, sponsored, or endorsed by Strava.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, GPS routes, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To turn your workouts into points and show your weekly progress.
  • To show your company's weekly leaderboard.
  • To let you redeem points for gift cards and deliver them to you.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems.

We process your information because you consent when you connect your Strava account, and because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for. We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Transfers outside South Africa

Your information is stored and processed outside South Africa by the providers above. We use providers that are bound by data protection laws or contractual terms that give a level of protection similar to POPIA. By using Vitalium you agree to these transfers.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we immediately remove your name, username, profile details, profile picture links and Strava access tokens, revoke Vitalium's access to your Strava account, and remove you from leaderboards and statistics.

We keep your past workouts, points and reward records without your name, profile or Strava login, for gift card record-keeping and fraud prevention. These records still include Strava's activity reference numbers. We keep them for [RETENTION PERIOD] and then delete them.

See how to delete your account.

8. How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit, both between the app and our server and between our server and our database. Your sign-in details are kept in your phone's secure storage. Each person can only access their own account's data, and only administrators can see team statistics. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you and the Information Regulator if a breach affects you.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete your information;
  • object to how we process your information;
  • withdraw your consent, by deleting your account or revoking Vitalium's access in your Strava settings;
  • complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

To make a request, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We respond within 30 days.

10. Age

Vitalium is a workplace app for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18.
