What is deleted

  • Your name, username, profile picture links, city, country, sex, weight and bio.

  • Your place on leaderboards and in company statistics.

What is kept

Your past workouts (sport type, date, duration, average heart rate and Strava's activity reference number), points, and reward records are kept without your name, profile or Strava login. We keep them for gift card record-keeping and fraud prevention for [RETENTION PERIOD], then delete them.
